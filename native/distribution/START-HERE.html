Your little adventure starts here.

Sidequest beta · Apple Silicon Macs (M1 or newer) · macOS 14 or newer

1. Open Sidequest

Drag Sidequest into Applications, then open it from there. No Node, terminal, or developer tools needed. Create your adventurer and start with built-in stories. Your progress saves on your own Mac.

This private beta is not Apple-notarized. If macOS blocks the first launch, and you trust the person who sent you this copy, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and look for Open Anyway for Sidequest. Follow the Mac’s confirmation prompts. You should only need this once. Do not disable Gatekeeper or other security settings.

2. Add a local storyteller (optional)

  1. Download and install LM Studio.
  2. Open the Qwen3 4B Instruct 2507 model page, then choose “Use Model in LM Studio.” Download the MLX 4-bit version, or Q4_K_M GGUF, and load it in LM Studio.
  3. In LM Studio’s Developer tab, start the local server, normally on port 1234. Keep “Serve on Local Network” off. Leave LM Studio running.
  4. In Sidequest, open World → the connections step. Click Find local providers, select LM Studio and the model, enable model stories, then Preview my story and save.

We suggest 16 GB memory. An 8 GB Mac may work with this small model; close other heavy apps and keep context modest (4096 is a starting point). Model download and first load can take a few minutes. Internet is needed to download LM Studio and the model; local storytelling can then run offline.

3. Bring your calendars (optional)

In Sidequest, open Agenda → Connect Google Calendar to connect directly to Google. The app includes one-time Google Cloud setup instructions: enable Google Calendar API, create a Desktop app OAuth client, download its JSON, then import it into Sidequest. Sign in with Google in your browser and choose your calendars. Credentials stay in macOS Keychain.

Or choose “Use Mac calendars instead” for accounts already added in Mac Calendar. Today’s timed meetings automatically become quests. All-day events stay optional, and completing a quest never edits your calendar.

If it needs a nudge

No models listed? Make sure the model is downloaded, loaded, and the LM Studio local server is running. Then click Find local providers again. Using a different server port? Enter it under advanced connection settings. Built-in stories keep working if the model is unavailable.

Linear is optional. You can add your own quests immediately. This package contains no sender’s saved progress, account keys, or model files.
